Netherwood - commissioning snag summary

December 2024 (compiled retrospectively - weekly records
incomplete for Oct/Nov)

Energisation achieved 09/12/2024, against a baseline of
30/09/2024. Total slip approximately 10 weeks.

Principal causes, in order of impact:

1. Planning condition discharge (6 weeks). Construction traffic
   management plan delayed by the civils contractor. Start on
   site pushed accordingly.

2. Transformer delivery (3 weeks). Supplier revised lead time
   after order placed.

3. Commissioning snags (1 week). Various, mostly minor. Notable:
   PCS firmware was not updated consistently across all units
   during snagging - one unit was offline during the update window
   and was missed. This was not picked up at handover.

LESSONS
The first two causes were both visible months in advance and
neither was formally escalated until they had already bitten. Our
weekly reports recorded them as "issues" from early on but there
is no mechanism that turns a repeated issue into an escalation.

Handover documentation to Operations was incomplete. The firmware
point above was known to the commissioning team and did not make
it into the handover pack.
