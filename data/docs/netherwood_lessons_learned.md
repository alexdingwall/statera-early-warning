# Netherwood - Lessons learned workshop

**Date:** 5 February 2025
**Attendees:** Project delivery, Development, Operations,
Procurement

## What went well
- Construction quality, no significant defects
- Site team / contractor relationship
- HSE record: zero reportable incidents

## What did not

**1. Early warnings were recorded but not escalated.**
Both the planning condition delay and the transformer lead time
appear in weekly reports weeks or months before they affected the
programme. Nobody connected the repetition. The weekly report
format records issues but has no concept of "this is the fifth
week running".

**2. Contractor performance information is not shared between
projects.**
The civils contractor's delay at Netherwood was not known to the
Drumgarve team when they were appointed there.

**3. Handover was incomplete.**
See separate handover status note. Operations inherited open items
with no clear owner.

## Agreed actions
1. Introduce a formal escalation trigger for repeated issues -
   owner: project delivery. **Status at time of writing: not done.**
2. Share contractor performance notes across projects - owner:
   procurement. **Status: not done.**
3. Handover checklist with sign-off - owner: operations.
   **Status: drafted.**
