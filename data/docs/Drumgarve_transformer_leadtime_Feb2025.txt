DRUMGARVE BESS - PROCUREMENT ISSUE

Date raised: 2025-02-07
Raised by: Procurement
Project: Drumgarve (40MW)

Main transformer lead time has been revised by the supplier from
38 weeks to 54 weeks. Notified by email 05/02, no advance warning.

This pushes delivery from September 2025 to January 2026.

IMPACT
Energisation forecast moves from Nov 2025 to Mar 2026, a slip of
approximately 16 weeks. Drumgarve does not currently hold a
capacity market agreement so the direct revenue exposure is lower
than it would be at Ashfield, but we lose a winter of trading
revenue.

OPTIONS CONSIDERED
1. Accept the revised date
2. Source from an alternative supplier - lead times quoted
   between 45 and 60 weeks, so no benefit
3. Second-hand / ex-stock unit - one identified, specification
   does not match

RECOMMENDATION
Accept and replan. Also: this is the second transformer lead time
extension from this supplier in 14 months (see Netherwood, Dec
2023). We should stop treating their quoted lead times as firm
and build contingency into the programme at the point of order,
not when they tell us.
