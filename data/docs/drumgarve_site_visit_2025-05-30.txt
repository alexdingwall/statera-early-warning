Drumgarve site visit - notes
30 May 2025

Pre-construction walkover with the civils contractor and the
landowner.

Points noted:

- Access track from the B-road is narrower than the drawings
  indicate at two pinch points. Will not accommodate a container
  delivery without widening. Contractor says "we'll manage" - I
  am not convinced. Same issue caused a second crane visit at
  Ashfield.

- Drainage: standing water at the northern end of the site after
  recent rain. Ground investigation report did not flag this.
  Worth a second look before foundation design is finalised.

- Landowner raised the hedgerow removal timing. Nesting season
  restrictions may affect the planned start date. Not currently
  in the programme risk register.

- Contractor has not yet provided the swept path analysis for the
  traffic management condition. Chased on site.

General impression: site is workable but there are three or four
things here that are not in the risk register and should be.
